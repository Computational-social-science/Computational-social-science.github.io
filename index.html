Recognition and credit in science

How did a name for unequal recognition enter the scientific record? This investigation traces the printed evidence for Robert K. Merton’s naming and the role of Harriet Zuckerman’s interviews in the scientific account.

19 May 1964 Earliest public print located in this investigation

What the record shows

Public naming
The 19 May 1964 issue of The Statesman attributes the naming to Merton. The exact date of the lecture and absolute priority remain open.
Research contributions
Merton formulated and explained the effect. Zuckerman identified recurring patterns in Nobel laureates’ accounts and probed specific cases. Her interviews supplied essential evidence.
Publication credit
Merton was the sole named author of the 1968 paper. He later wrote that both researchers merited the byline.
